HOW TO CLEAR STORAGE ON ANDROID
1. Open AI Cleaner and tap Smart Clean, or pick Photos, Videos, Contacts or Events.
2. Review the groups: duplicates, similar photos, blurry photos, screenshots, large files.
3. Select what to remove and confirm. Deleted photos and videos go to your phone's Trash, so you can restore them (30 days by default, configurable).
