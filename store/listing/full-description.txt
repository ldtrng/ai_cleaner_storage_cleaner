SAFE BY DESIGN

• Light and dark themes

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
• Photos and videos: to scan your whole gallery for duplicates, blurry shots and large files. The Android photo picker can't do this, because the scan must cover the whole library.
• Contacts and Calendar: only if you open those cleaners.
• Usage access: only for App Manager, to show app size and last-used time.
